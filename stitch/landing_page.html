TECHNICAL SUITE

Core Platform Capabilities

A modular engine architected for signals intelligence (SIGINT), electronic defense, and SDR protocol reverse engineering.

01

Signal Visualization

Interactive multi-domain visualizations including In-Phase/Quadrature time domain, FFT Power Spectrum, Spectrogram Heatmaps, and constellation clustering.

02

AI Parameter Detection

Automated estimation of carrier frequency offset, baud rate, signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), and active bandwidth utilizing deep convolutional neural networks.

03

Modulation Analysis

Comprehensive classifier supporting 2FSK, 4FSK, BPSK, QPSK, 8PSK, 16QAM, 64QAM with automated constellation diagram alignment and EVM metrics.

04

De-interleaving Engine

Algorithmic detection and reconstruction of interleaved data patterns including matrix Block Interleaving, Convolutional Interleaving, and Helical structures.

05

FEC Processing

Hardware-accelerated decoding pipelines for Viterbi (1/2, 2/3, 3/4 rates), Reed-Solomon RS(255, 223/239), and Low-Density Parity-Check (LDPC) forward error correction.

06

Bit Stream Correlation

Cross-correlation search for sync preambles, Barker codes, frame headers, CRC polynomial auto-detection, and raw hexadecimal bit-slicing.
